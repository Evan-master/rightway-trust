How RightWay AI Uses Google Sign-In & Gmail APIs

RightWay AI integrates with Google OAuth 2.0 strictly on an opt-in basis when you choose to sign in with Google or connect your Gmail account inside our native desktop client:

  • Account Sign-In (openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile): Authenticates your identity and displays your name and email address in the application interface.
  • Email Productivity & Client Actions (gmail.modify, gmail.compose, gmail.send): Used exclusively when you explicitly connect Gmail inside the Polaris Agent macOS desktop app so you can search and read threads, update labels (such as marking messages read or archiving), create drafts for review, and send user-approved replies.
  • Local Keychain Security: Desktop Gmail OAuth tokens are encrypted locally in your computer's macOS Keychain and are never stored on RightWay AI servers.
  • Google API Limited Use Compliance: RightWay AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never sell user data, never use Google user data for advertising, and never use Gmail data to train generalized AI models.
